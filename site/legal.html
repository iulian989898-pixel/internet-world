Who operates this site?

Impressum / legal notice: Operator name, postal address, contact email and other details required under applicable German law must be supplied by the operator. These details are currently pending.

What is stored?

To allow you to resume the quiz and discourage duplicate answers from the same browser, the site stores a random anonymous identifier in your browser's localStorage. The voting database keeps a SHA-256 digest of that identifier alongside the option you selected for each question. The database also stores global vote totals and your final score, linked to a randomly generated result ID. No name, email address or user-submitted text is requested.

Result links can be shared publicly. Anyone who receives a result link can see the saved score, title, number of majority matches and comparison statistics, but not your individual answers or random browser identifier.

Infrastructure providers such as Netlify may process technical data (including IP addresses) in order to deliver the site, secure it and operate server logs. We do not intentionally use advertising cookies, cross-site analytics, or external fonts in this prototype. The operator must confirm actual service configurations and legal bases before launch.

What does the score mean?

The Normality Score is entertainment only. It is a comparison with the current most popular answers, not a medical, psychological, intelligence, or personality assessment. Percentages and saved result statistics can differ over time as more people vote.

What if you do not want to participate?

You can browse this legal information without voting. If you choose to vote, your browser stores the identifier needed for session continuity. Disabling local storage can affect session continuity. Clearing local storage removes the local identifier, but it does not automatically delete aggregated votes or a previously shared result stored by the service.

Contact & data rights

The operator must provide an operational contact address and a process for privacy requests before public launch. Do not use the service to disclose personal or sensitive information.
