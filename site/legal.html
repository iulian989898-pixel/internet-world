What is stored?

To help you resume the quiz and discourage duplicate voting from the same browser, this site stores a random identifier in your browser's localStorage. While your quiz is in progress, the database holds a SHA-256 digest of that identifier and your selected options. Once you finish, those individual answers are deleted from the vote records; only the aggregated global totals and your saved result remain. Incomplete voting records older than 30 days are removed by a daily cleanup job. The saved result retains the session digest, normality score, title, comparison statistics, and an unguessable result ID. No name, email address, or free-text submission is requested.

During a voluntary replay, your choices are temporarily kept in browser localStorage so the replay can survive a page refresh. Replay votes are not added to global voting totals. When the replay finishes, all 20 choices are sent securely to the server for score calculation, but are not stored as individual vote records; the saved final replay result remains available through its share link. Your browser removes the temporary replay choices after a successful finish. Result links can be shared publicly. Anyone who receives a result link can see the saved score, title, number of majority matches and comparison statistics, but not your individual answers or random browser identifier.

Infrastructure providers such as Netlify may process technical data (including IP addresses) in order to deliver the site, secure it and operate server logs. The app does not intentionally install advertising cookies, cross-site analytics, or external fonts. The session identifier is sent in the body of HTTPS requests to the API, not as a URL query parameter. The operator must confirm actual service configurations and legal bases before launch.

What does the score mean?

The Normality Score is entertainment only. It is a comparison with the current most popular answers, not a medical, psychological, intelligence, or personality assessment. Percentages and saved result statistics can differ over time as more people vote.

What if you do not want to participate?

You can browse this legal information without voting. If you choose to vote, your browser stores a random identifier needed for session continuity. Disabling localStorage can affect the ability to resume. Clearing localStorage removes the local identifier on your device, but does not automatically delete already aggregated votes or a previously shared result. Saved result links are intended to remain accessible until deleted by the site operator; they are public to anyone with the link.
